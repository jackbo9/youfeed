# YouFeed Exhibition Field Guide / Reconstruction v1.1


# Help visitors share their views

Before the event: sections 01–03 and 08. During a shift: 04–06. Record and hand over issues: 07.

About this guide

Recreated from a participant’s recollections and current project materials; not the historical original. Area tasks, questions, prompts and field rules are newly proposed. The screenshot shows the later concept demo.

Service flow / The final stage belongs to the backend team

Staff invite > Visitor opens the correct entry > Gives feedback > Reviews and finishes > Backend receives and analyses.

This diagram shows the service relationship. The current mobile demo captures no audio, sends no feedback and is not connected to the company platform or InsightGPT.

An invitation / Explain the purpose, then offer a choice

“We’re collecting views on the exhibition, products and sessions. If you’d like to take part, tap this NFC card with your phone or scan the QR code to share your thoughts. Taking part is entirely up to you.”

Who does what

Visitor: chooses whether to join, what to say, what to edit and when to stop.
Staff: checks the subject and explains the task and controls; does not answer or rewrite feedback for visitors.
Field lead: checks entries, coordinates issues and accepts handovers.
Backend team: checks receipt and system issues. Analysis design is outside this guide.

For demo training, add: “This is a simulation. Voice uses sample text and nothing is sent.” Do not promise anonymity, a fixed duration, retention periods or a response time.

# Invite. Enter. Express. Review.

The screenshot explains the controls. It is the current R7 concept demo, not the historical event interface.

[R7 demo screenshot](../../screenshots/r7-home-390.png)

01  Invite / Participation is optional

Use the invitation in section 01. If the visitor declines or wants to leave, stop without asking them to justify it.

02  Enter / Confirm the subject

Ask the visitor to check the product or session name. If NFC does not respond, use a verified QR code for the same subject. Actual NFC / QR links are not verified by this repository.

03  Express / Let the visitor choose

Try speaking is the simulated voice entry. Write a thought opens text input. Visitors can write directly; there is no need to try voice first.

04  Review / The visitor owns the words

Ask whether the text says what they mean. Edit changes the example; Continue as text keeps the existing words for editing. Explain controls, but do not rewrite their opinion.

05  Finish / A finished page is not proof of receipt

Demo complete means only that the demo ended; the page states nothing was sent. At a live event, use the agreed completion indicator. If the result is unclear, do not claim success or immediately resubmit. See section 06.

Protect what has already been written

In the demo, switching views keeps existing text; refreshing or restarting clears it. A new voice sample can replace the draft. Explain any clear or re-record confirmation first, then let the visitor decide.


# Support their own words

Listen first. Offer one prompt. If they do not want to add anything, accept their answer or let them finish.

### 01 What is YouFeed?
Aim: Explain purpose and choice.
Say: “It’s a way to share your views about this place. You can choose whether you’d like to take part.”
Avoid: No promises of anonymity, timing, rewards or action on every comment.

### 02 What should I comment on?
Aim: Confirm and narrow the subject.
Say: “This page is about [subject]. You can share just one thing that stood out. A first impression is fine.”
Avoid: Do not suggest an answer or say “You could praise the design.”

### 03 “No comments” / “It’s fine”
Aim: Offer one optional follow-up.
Say: “If you’d like, what made you feel that way? It’s also fine to leave it there.”
Avoid: Do not turn vague words into praise, keep probing or require more words.

### 04 Voice is inconvenient
Aim: Make writing a direct choice.
Say: “You can choose text and write it in your own way.”
Avoid: No forced microphone access or speaking for them. The demo does not record.

### 05 Wrong product or session
Aim: Pause and check the subject.
Say: “This page shows [page subject]. Did you mean [target subject]? Let’s check the entry first.”
Avoid: No wrong-page entry or automatic draft transfer. Explain loss risks first.

### 06 They want to stop
Aim: Respect the decision to leave.
Say: “Of course. Thank you. You can stop whenever you like.”
Avoid: No pressure, private questions or finishing for them. Closing is not deletion.


# Check the subject, then recover

[UI] = current demo. [TBC] = unverified live capability. All other recovery and stop rules are proposed for the field team.

First: have they written or pressed submit?
Nothing written > Check entry / connection; A draft exists > Do not refresh; protect text; Submitted / unclear > Do not resubmit. See E6.

E1 / Unstable network or page will not open

SEE / Endless loading, a blank page or connection error.

CHECK / Ask whether they have written anything or pressed submit. Do not refresh a draft. Check the entry and connection.

TRY / With no draft, the visitor may choose another available network and reopen the verified entry. For a draft or prior submit, see E6.

STOP / After one changed recovery attempt fails, or the visitor wants to stop. Do not repeatedly refresh.

HAND OFF / Log time, area, entry, error and outcome to the lead. If the same entry fails on multiple devices, pause invitations there.

E2 / NFC or QR code does not open

SEE / No response to tapping, unreadable QR code or failed redirect.

CHECK / Check the subject on the card, any obstruction and the destination. Do not ask visitors to install an unknown app.

TRY / Use a verified QR code for the same subject after NFC fails. If QR fails, use only a backup link verified by the lead.

STOP / If the alternative fails or none is verified. Do not invent links or use another product’s entry.

HAND OFF / Log touchpoint ID, NFC / QR, symptom and result to the lead for material and route checks.

E3 / The page shows the wrong subject

SEE / The name, image or session differs from the intended feedback subject.

CHECK / Compare the product / sign, card and page; pause writing.

TRY / With no draft, open the verified correct entry. If text exists, explain possible loss before the visitor chooses whether to continue.

STOP / If the correct entry is still wrong or cannot be found. Do not send feedback to the wrong subject.

HAND OFF / Log entry ID, shown and intended subject. Lead pauses the material; technical support checks mapping. [TBC] Draft transfer.


# Unclear result? Do not resubmit.

[UI] = current simulation. [TBC] = live capability to confirm. Other recovery and stop rules are proposed for the field team.

First: what does the page show?
Voice unavailable > Switch to text. See E4.; Explicit error > Safe to retry? See E5.; No clear result > Stop resubmitting. See E6.

E4 / Voice unavailable, empty or not transcribed

SEE / Voice unavailable, No words picked up, or a transcription error.

CHECK / [UI] Check for a simulated label. This demo does not request microphone permission, record sound or transcribe audio.

TRY / [UI] Write instead keeps earlier text. Try voice again / Try again tests simulated recovery.

STOP / After one unsuccessful recovery attempt, or if they prefer not to speak: write or finish. Do not keep requesting permission.

HAND OFF / Log the message and whether switching helped to the lead. [TBC] Technical support checks live permissions and compatibility.

E5 / The page explicitly reports a submit error

SEE / An error is shown with a retry option.

CHECK / [UI] The demo keeps the draft for retry. This does not prove a live system has not already received a submission.

TRY / Use the lead’s confirmed interpretation of live errors. Let the visitor retry only when it is known to be safe.

STOP / After one safe retry fails, or receipt becomes uncertain. Move to E6.

HAND OFF / Log error, time, retry count and last state to the lead / technical support. [TBC] Timeout meaning and duplicate handling.

E6 / Completion is unclear

SEE / Still waiting, page closed, connection lost or no clear completion message.

CHECK / No success message does not mean nothing was received. Ask what happened last and whether submit was pressed.

TRY / Keep the page if still open; do not refresh or reopen to resubmit. Let the visitor leave. The lead asks the backend team to check.

STOP / When no confirmed mechanism can establish the outcome. Record “unclear”; do not ask the visitor to wait indefinitely.

HAND OFF / Log time, entry, last action and page state; receipt ID only if provided. Lead hands over to backend. The demo has no live receipt lookup.

Who receives it / Close the loop [proposed]

Staff hand records to the field lead. Entry, network and voice issues go to technical support; receipt checks to the backend team; subject names, sessions and wording to the area content owner. Fill actual contacts in section 08.

Escalate repeated entry failures, wrong subjects or unexpected personal information immediately. Hand over other records at the agreed shift interval. The recipient accepts the issue, sets a follow-up time and records closure evidence; forwarding is not closure.

Collect only what troubleshooting needs. Do not keep visitor feedback, audio or identifiable screenshots on personal devices. If a screenshot is necessary, the lead confirms the method and unrelated details are hidden. Agree storage, access and retention first.
