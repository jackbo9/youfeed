# Help visitors share their views

Before the event: 01-03 and 08. During a shift: 04-06 or the quick sheet. Use 07 for records.

About this guide

Reconstructed from project experience and recollections, 2026. The exhibition, product and talk/session contexts are recalled from the project. Specific questions, prompts and operating rules are newly proposed. The original handbook is unavailable.

Service relationship and roles

STAFF → Invite

VISITOR → Open the right entry

VISITOR → Give feedback

VISITOR → Review and finish

BACKEND → Receive / analyse

Explain the purpose, then offer a choice

“We would like your views on [this area / product / session]. If you wish, tap the NFC card or scan the QR code to share them by voice or text. Taking part is entirely optional.”

Who does what

Visitor / Chooses whether to join, what to say, what to edit and when to stop.

Staff / Checks the subject and explains the task and controls; does not answer or rewrite.

Field lead / Checks entries, coordinates issues and accepts follow-up records.

Backend / Checks receipt and system issues; owns subsequent analysis.

Use the agreed purpose statement. Do not invent promises of anonymity, rewards, timing or responses. Demo training notes are in section 08.

# Connect prompts to controls

Staff explain controls; visitors own the words. Crops show the current interface demo.

Invite using section 01. Check the product or session name on entry, then let the visitor choose how to respond.

01 / Let the visitor choose

Try speaking opens voice; Write a thought opens text. Writing is a direct choice when speaking is inconvenient.

02 / Review their own words

Ask whether the text says what they mean. Edit changes the text; Continue as text keeps it for editing. Explain the buttons without rewriting their opinion.

03 / Read the completion state

Use the agreed completion indicator. Demo complete in this screenshot means the simulation ended. For uncertain live receipt, follow E6.

Before clearing, re-recording or restarting

Explain that content may be replaced or lost, then let the visitor decide. Do not refresh, clear or submit for them just to recover the interface.


# Support their own words

Listen first. Offer one neutral prompt. Accept the answer, or let the visitor finish.

## 01 What is YouFeed?
Explain purpose and choice.
It is a way to share your views about this place. You can choose whether to take part.
Use the agreed purpose; do not promise rewards or action on every comment.

## 02 What should I comment on?
Confirm and narrow the subject.
This page is about [subject]. You can share one thing that stood out.
Do not suggest answers or ask them to praise the design.

## 03 “No comments” / “It’s fine”
Offer one optional follow-up.
If you wish, what made you feel that way? It is fine to leave it there.
Do not keep probing or turn vague words into praise.

## 04 Voice is inconvenient
Offer writing directly.
You can choose text and write in your own way.
Do not insist on voice, record or answer for them.

## 05 Wrong product or session
Pause and check the entry.
Did you mean [target subject]? Let us check the entry first.
Do not use the wrong page. Explain loss risks if a draft exists.

## 06 They want to stop
Respect the decision to leave.
Of course. Thank you. You can stop whenever you like.
No pressure, probing for reasons or finishing for them.


# Check the state before recovery

First ask: is there a draft? Has submit been pressed? Do not refresh every error.

Nothing written → Check entry / connection

Draft, not submitted → Keep page. See E1.

Submitted / uncertain → Do not resubmit. E6.

E1 / Unstable network or page will not open

ASK / Is there a draft? Has submit been pressed? Then check the connection and entry.

NO DRAFT / The visitor may choose another network and reopen the verified entry.

DRAFT ONLY / Keep the page; do not refresh or clear. Explain it has not been submitted. Use an agreed draft-recovery method, or let the visitor stop. Do not route to a receipt check.

SUBMITTED / If submit was pressed or this is uncertain, follow E6. Do not reopen to resubmit.

STOP / LOG / Stop after one changed recovery attempt fails, or if the visitor wishes. Log entry, state and outcome to the lead.

E2 / NFC or QR code does not open

CHECK / Check the card subject, any QR obstruction and the target link.

TRY / If NFC fails, use a verified QR for the same subject. If QR fails, use only a backup entry verified by the lead.

STOP / LOG / Stop if the alternative fails or none exists. Log touchpoint ID and symptom to the lead for material checks.

E3 / The page shows the wrong subject

CHECK / Compare the product / sign, material and page; pause writing.

TRY / With no draft, open the correct entry. If text exists, explain possible loss; do not promise automatic transfer.

STOP / LOG / If the right entry is unavailable or still wrong, pause the material. Log shown and intended subjects to the lead.


# Unclear receipt? Do not resubmit.

Follow the visible state. Invite a retry only when it is confirmed safe.

Voice unavailable → Switch to text. E4.

Explicit error → Safe to retry? E5.

Unclear receipt → Do not resubmit. E6.

E4 / Voice unavailable or transcription fails

CHECK / Read the message and check whether any text already exists.

TRY / Offer text and follow the page instructions to retain existing words. Try voice recovery only if supported by the confirmed version.

STOP / LOG / After one failed recovery, or if they prefer, write or finish. Log the message and outcome to the lead.

E5 / An explicit submit error

CHECK / Read the error using the lead’s agreed interpretation. An error may not mean the backend received nothing.

TRY / Let the visitor retry only if that error is confirmed safe to retry.

STOP / LOG / After one failed safe retry, or if receipt is uncertain, follow E6. Log time, retry count and last state to the lead.

E6 / Submitted, but receipt is unclear

CHECK / Ask what happened last and read the page. No success message does not prove nothing was received.

ON SITE / Keep the page if open; do not refresh or reopen to resubmit. Explain the uncertainty and let the visitor stop waiting.

STOP / LOG / Stop retrying. Log time, entry, last action and any receipt ID. The lead asks the backend to check.

STATUS / Record “unclear” and update after checking. Without a confirmed mechanism, do not claim receipt.

The lead confirms recovery rules in section 08. Do not promise unverified capabilities.
